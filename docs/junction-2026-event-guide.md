## Rules (prize eligibility)

1. No code written before the event. All code except open-source libraries must be written at the event. Working on something pre-built for this idea, or on a project tied to an existing company, means disqualification.
2. Team size 1 to 4.
3. Take part in at least one challenge.
4. The whole team must take part in live peer review on Sunday to be eligible for the main prize.
5. The submission must include a pitch video of **at most 2 minutes**. They say they will be strict about this.
6. Submit a source code link. All submissions are licensed **MIT**, and organizers must be able to access the code.

IP: participants own their work (MIT-licensed). Partner APIs remain subject to the partner's terms.

## Judging

- **Criteria:** Innovation (new and different from existing ideas), Impact (size and share of the affected people helped), Implementation (realistic, viable, feasible). Each partner also sets its own criteria for its challenge, so read those closely.
- **Challenge winners** are picked by the partners.
- **Overall winner:** peer review picks the top 5. The top 5 each pitch 3 minutes on stage (mics are cut at time) followed by 3 minutes of Q&A, and a jury of 7 judges votes. One handheld mic per team. You can demo from your own laptop, and they check the connection beforehand. There's no practice slot after review, so rehearse before then.
- During peer review you review the projects assigned to you. You can mark a team "not present".

## FAQ highlights (2026.hackjunction.com)

- **AI-focused?** Not AI-only, but most 2026 challenges are AI: agents, software, and physical AI and robotics in the Hardware Lab.
- **Getting there:** Hype Areena is in Kivenlahti, Espoo, at the western end of the metro line. About 30 min by metro from Helsinki Central, about 30 min by car from Helsinki-Vantaa Airport.
- **Sleeping:** open 24/7 but there are no sleeping areas, and the guide says sleeping there is not legally allowed. Book a room.
- **Travel grants:** the €300 and €100 grants are closed. Don't count on one unless you got written confirmation.
- **Ownership:** you own your project, published under MIT, so partners and anyone else may use and build on it with your team credited.
- **Recruiting:** partners recruit on site. Investors attend on a paid Investor Pass, so expect VCs and angels on the floor and at the top 5 pitches.
